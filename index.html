01 / ABOUT ME

a few things about me

About me.

Heya!! I’m a person who likes taking a complicated problem and making something useful out of it.

That might mean an agent workflow, a real-time speech integration, or tracing a stubborn bug until the whole thing finally clicks. I build things, break things (well, strategically), and care about what happens after the demo.

My work lives where AI, software, and people meet. I like the messy middle: turning an idea into a working system, testing the assumptions, and helping someone else feel confident using it.

Outside the code? Sketches, half-finished experiments, and a proud collection of participation ribbons. There’s a whole page for that ↗
